Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can save and share text without an account. Log in to keep a text under your account, so only you can change it.

Are my texts private?

No. Anyone with the link can read a text, even one saved under an account. Don't save passwords or other sensitive information.

What happens when a text expires?

It's deleted and its link stops working. Texts set to never expire stay until someone deletes them.

Can I change the expiry later?

Yes. Open the text, pick a new option, and save. The countdown restarts from that moment. Editing a text without changing the option keeps the original countdown.

What does View raw do?

It opens the text on its own as plain text, with no page around it. That's useful for copying or downloading it.

Is SaveText free?

Yes, saving and sharing text is free.
