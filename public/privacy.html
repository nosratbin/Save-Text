Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

What we store

  • Texts: the title, content, and expiry you choose.
  • Accounts: your username, a hash of your password, and the date you signed up. We don't ask for your email address or real name.
  • Login token: kept in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in. Logging out removes it.
  • Server logs: record the ID and title of texts when they are saved or deleted.

Who can see your texts

Anyone with the link can read a text, even one saved under an account. Texts saved without an account also appear in the Public texts list, and anyone can edit or delete them. Only the account owner can change a text saved under an account.

Don't save passwords or other sensitive information on SaveText.

How long we keep texts

Texts with an expiry are deleted after that time. Texts set to never expire stay until someone deletes them.

Cookies, analytics and ads

SaveText doesn't currently set cookies, use analytics, or show ads. We'll update this page before adding any of them.

Services we use

  • Render hosts the site and handles requests, which includes your IP address.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores texts and accounts.
  • Google Fonts and Tailwind CSS load fonts and styles from their own servers, so they also receive your IP address.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
