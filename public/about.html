About SaveText

SaveText is a simple online notepad. Paste some text, save it, and you get a link you can open on any device or send to someone else.

How it works

  1. Type or paste your text and give it a title.
  2. Choose when it expires: 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 1 year, or never.
  3. Save it and share the link. Anyone with the link can read the text or open the raw version.

What you can use it for

  • Moving text between your phone and computer
  • Sharing a code snippet, log, or config file
  • Sending notes that should disappear after a set time
  • Keeping a quick draft you can come back to

With or without an account

You can start saving right away, with no sign-up. Create an account if you want your texts listed in one place and want to be the only one who can change them.
